# Playtest round 1: plain build

2026-11-05. One subagent (Sonnet), told nothing about the game, playing from screenshots in a copy of the project with a
neutral folder name and no docs. Launch: no flags. The report below is the agent's, shortened.
Stills of the same build: `docs/demo/plain_*.png`.

## What it did

| # | Input | Why | Expected | Actual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | none, 2 s and 6 s | first look | | A cartoon cat with a worried mouth, a faint grey word "fish" bottom left. No title, no score, no buttons. The two frames look the same. |
| 1 | tap the cat | the only object on screen | a purr, a jump, a heart | "Almost no visible change." The cat moved about 1 px, which could be idle breathing. |
| 2 | tap "fish" | to see what it does | a fish appears, the cat eats, a menu opens | "No fish, no menu, no text change." The cat sat slightly lower; it could not tell whether that was the tap or the breathing. |
| 3 | hold on empty space 2 s, swipe across, then wait to 22 s | try other gestures, let time pass | some change | Same as the start at 6 s. At 22 s the eyes were closed lines. "I could not tell" whether that was idle animation, a blink or a reaction. |

## What it reported

- Pressable things that did not visibly react: the cat, "fish", the empty lower half.
- Things it later realized were pressable: nothing confirmed.
- Goal and rules: "I could not tell." Its guess: a pet that gets hungry or sleepy over time, and "fish" is probably a
  feed action, "but it did not respond to my tap".
- Most lost: right after tapping "fish". "I could not tell whether the tap missed or the game just had no response. The
  label is also very faint, so I could not tell whether it is a button at all."

## What was really going on (checked by the person, not the agent)

"Fish" **did** work. Fed at 5, 10 and 13 s, the cat still has open eyes at 15 s; unfed, they are closed
(`docs/demo/plain_fed_vs_unfed.png`). The agent could not tell because feeding changed a number nobody could see. So the
bug is not that the button is broken. It is three missing things:

1. Nothing says what the goal is.
2. Nothing shows hunger, the one number that decides whether you lose.
3. Pressing the fish gives no motion, sound or change on screen.

And "fish" in faint grey text did not look pressable. The idle breathing made it worse: a constant small motion the
agent could not tell apart from a reaction.

## What was changed afterwards (the `--clear` build)

- A how-to-play card before the first play: goal, what the bar means, what to press and when.
- A hunger bar and a seconds counter, always visible.
- The feed control is an orange button, and it pulses once hunger passes 0.6.
- Feeding pops the cat.
- The loss message says what to do next ("Tap to try again").
