# Playtest round 1: plain build

2026-11-04. One subagent (Sonnet), told nothing about the game, playing from screenshots. Launch: no flags.
Screenshots it saw: `docs/demo/plain_*.png`. The report below is the agent's, shortened.

## What it did

| # | Input | Why | Expected | Actual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | none, 2 s and 6 s | first look | | Cat face, glum mouth, a faint word "fish". Nothing changes. |
| 1 | tap "fish" | the only text on screen | fish appears, cat reacts | Screen byte-identical to the first one. |
| 2 | tap the cat | maybe it purrs | a reaction | Nothing. |
| 3 | swipe across the face | petting | a reaction | Nothing. |
| 4 | hold on the face 2.5 s | | a reaction | Nothing. |
| 5 | tap empty space | | | Nothing. |
| 6 | no input, wait 20 s | | | Eyes turned to lines at about 16 s, then "The cat left." |
| 7 | tap "fish" at 1 s, tap the cat at 3 s and 5 s, wait | does feeding delay it | | Looked the same as with no input. |

## What it reported

- Pressable things that did not react: "fish", the cat's face, empty space.
- Things it later realized were pressable: **nothing**.
- Goal and rules: "I could not tell."
- Most lost: right after the first tap on "fish", when nothing happened. "Everything stayed static for about 15 s, and
  then the game seemed to end by itself."

## What was really going on (checked by the person, not the agent)

"Fish" **did** work. Fed at 5, 10 and 13 s, the cat still has open eyes at 15 s; unfed, they are closed
(`docs/demo/plain_fed_vs_unfed.png`). The agent concluded "nothing responds" because feeding changed a number nobody
could see. So the bug is not that the button is broken. It is three missing things:

1. Nothing says what the goal is.
2. Nothing shows hunger, the one number that decides whether you lose.
3. Pressing the fish gives no motion, sound or change on screen.

And "fish" in faint grey text did not look pressable.

## What was changed afterwards (the `--clear` build)

- A how-to-play card before the first play: goal, what the bar means, what to press and when.
- A hunger bar and a seconds counter, always visible.
- The feed control is an orange button with a border, and it pulses once hunger passes 0.6.
- Feeding pops the cat.
- The loss message says what to do next ("Tap to try again").
