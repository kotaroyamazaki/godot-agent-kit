# Playtest round 2: `--clear` build

2026-11-05. A **new** subagent (Sonnet), told nothing about the game, playing from screenshots in a neutral copy.
Launch: `-- --clear`. The report below is the agent's, shortened. Stills of the same build: `docs/demo/clear_*.png`.

## What it did

| # | Input | Why | Expected | Actual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | none, 2 s and 6 s | first look | | A how-to-play card over the screen; behind it a bar, "0 / 30 s", the cat's ears, a dimmed FEED FISH button. OK and FEED FISH looked pressable. |
| 1 | tap OK | the card was blocking the screen | the game starts | The card closed, FEED FISH turned bright orange, the bar began to fill: green, then olive, then brownish-yellow. |
| 2 | OK, then wait | what happens if left alone | the bar fills, the cat reacts | Bar red and nearly full at 14 s on the counter, eyes closed. "The cat left. Tap to try again." at 18 s. |
| 3 | OK, then tap FEED FISH at 11.5 s | the card said to | the bar drops | The bar dropped back to short and green, the button lightened as it was pressed, the eyes were open. |

## What it reported

- Pressable things that did not react: none. It pressed only OK and FEED FISH, and both did something.
- Goal and rules, in its own words: keep the hunger bar from filling for 30 seconds; it fills over time and goes green,
  yellow, red; FEED FISH drops it back down; if it fills, the cat leaves and you lose. That is the rule set the game has.
- Most lost: "I was never badly lost, because the dialog explained the rules."
- **Still found:** "When the bar turns red, tap FEED FISH" left it unsure of the margin. "The bar was already mostly
  red at 14 s, only a few seconds before failure. I could not tell how much margin I have when it is red."

## What the round did not cover

- It never reached 30 s, so it did not see the win screen.
- It never tapped to retry, so "Tap to try again" is untested by a stranger.
- It noticed the counter runs about 2 s behind launch time. That is because the counter starts only after OK.

## Result

Round 1: could not tell the goal, never confirmed anything was pressable, lost right after pressing "fish".
Round 2: stated the goal and the rules correctly, found both controls at once, and still found one real problem: "red"
does not say how much time is left. That would be the next fix (for example, turn red earlier, or show seconds left).
